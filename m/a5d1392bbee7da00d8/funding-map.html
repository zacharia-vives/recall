If you only do three things

  1. imec.istart, before 30 September. Three days of writing for a one in six shot at 100,000 with no upfront equity, and they ask for exactly what you already have: a working proof of concept. The product is not the question here, the full-time commitment is.
  2. VLAIO Innovatieve starterssteun, the day after you incorporate. 50,000, no deadline, written for a young company doing something nobody else in the sector does. Best odds on the board.
  3. Erasmus+ KA210 through VIVES, October conversation for the March 2027 call. 30,000 as a lump sum with almost no reporting, and you already have the second country. It funds digital inclusion work around Recall, never the app itself, which is the part you cannot do alone anyway.
