The finding, and it is not the one in the business plan

Only one route on this list can pay for the build. Development is 65,000 and the lean year costs 45,000 on top, so a 30,000 or 50,000 grant does not get you to launch on its own, however good its odds are. imec.istart at 100,000 does, and it is the only single source that does.

So the plan is not one grant, it is a stack. imec.istart to build, VLAIO to breathe, Erasmus+ to do the part that is not the app. Tick them together in the panel and watch the trough get shallower and the crossing come earlier.

And break even never moves. 536 households while nobody takes a salary, 1,429 once somebody does. Every grant is time to reach that, not a way around it.
