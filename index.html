Your family would like to help

What that means. The cards you keep, their photos, what Recall reads off your letters and whether a reminder was done would be kept for your family as well, on a computer in Germany, not only on this phone.

Who can see them. Only the people your family has let in. You can see their names on your Today screen, and everything they do is written down where you can read it.

Some of it is private in a special way. Letters from a doctor or a hospital say things about your health, and papers from a lawyer or a bank say things about your money. The law treats those as needing your clear yes, and that is what this screen is.

You can say no. Recall keeps working on this phone on its own. Nothing goes anywhere.

You can stop later. Ask for the help screen and press stop sharing. What your family already has stays with them until they delete it, and nothing new is shared.

How does Recall work?

  1. Press Camera and point it at a letter or a photo.
  2. Recall makes the letters bigger and reads them out loud.
  3. Press Keep it. Recall keeps the date and puts it on your Today screen on the day.
